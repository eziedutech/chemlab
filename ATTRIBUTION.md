# Third-Party Assets

## 3D laboratory glassware

- **Work:** Chemistry Glassware
- **Author:** maxdragonn (https://sketchfab.com/maxdragon)
- **Source:** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/chemistry-glassware-b8594f7dc7e8442dbaaae7a11da4a962
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Used in:** the beaker and graduated cylinder meshes in the 3D lab scenes
- **Modifications:** unused meshes removed; third-party branding decals removed from the
  textures; re-exported to glTF/GLB; materials replaced with the project's own glass and
  liquid shaders.

All other 3D geometry in this project, the lab room and its benches and shelving, the
liquid volumes, the bubbles, the electrodes, the electrolyte test lamp, the spatula, and
the egg, is original work built from geometric primitives in code.
